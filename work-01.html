TWINK

Making everyday brushing easier for children

Twink is a smart toothbrush designed to make brushing more engaging and help children develop better brushing habits. It uses interactive feedback to guide brushing pressure and timing making the experience easier for children to understand and follow.

I designed Twink with Swasti Aggarwal as a college project, taking the product from identifying the problem through user research, concept development, prototyping to actual product development.
