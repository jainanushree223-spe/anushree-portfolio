THEATER

Turning fragrance browsing into guided discovery

Theater's existing perfume experience relied mainly on static category browsing, requiring users to move between collection pages to discover relevant products.

The fragrance discovery journey was made more engaging by introducing inline product exploration and a personalized quiz, helping users find relevant scents with less friction.
