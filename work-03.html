H-QWIK

Making healthcare visits feel less complicated

Healthcare visits can be confusing from finding the right department to keeping track of appointments and knowing what to do next. H-QWIK was designed as a concept to make the journey simpler, faster and more connected.

I designed H-QWIK independently as a college project, from identifying the problem through UX research, information architecture, user flows, wireframes, interface design and prototyping.
